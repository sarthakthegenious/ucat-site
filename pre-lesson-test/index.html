Instructions

  • Each question shows a reference time. You can take longer in this practice test; the timer just records how long you took.
  • For each question, write down the strategy or approach you used, either in the box under the question or on a piece of paper, and bring it to your first lesson.
  • A calculator, Flag for review and the Navigator are available, like the real test.
  • This test is meant to feel challenging. Most students find the pace difficult before learning the strategies. Do your best, and if a question takes too long, make your best guess and move on.
  • When you finish, we'll analyse your answers and give you feedback at your first lesson.
