What students who sat this year's UCAT reported
VR Most passages were 3–4 paragraphs with author/conclusion-type questions. Some question banks use longer passages that don't reflect real exam structure — don't over-prepare for those.
DM Logical puzzles were less straightforward than official banks. Syllogisms required strong pattern recognition across All/Some/None combinations — vague statements, not clean-cut ones.
QR Higher frequency of mean/median/mode and percentage questions. Efficient skipping on calculation-heavy questions was the key differentiator — don't burn time on QR outliers.
SJT Notably manageable for students with a clear grasp of primary and secondary ethical values. Framework understanding beat memorisation every time.
KEY The biggest mistake: treating more questions as better preparation. Without a locked-in, consistent strategy, scores fluctuate wildly. Strategy first, volume second.
